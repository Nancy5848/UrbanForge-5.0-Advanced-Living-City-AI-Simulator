Build Anything, Anywhere – Drag and drop from 33+ unique SVG items including houses, skyscrapers, parks, shops, vehicles, people, and animals to design your dream city.
Real-Time Economy Engine – Every building has income and upkeep. Watch your budget grow or struggle with deficits every 10 seconds (1 game day).
Dynamic Day & Night Cycle – Experience 4 beautiful times of day (Morning, Afternoon, Evening, Night) with changing sky colors, building window glows, and ambient music.
4 Living Seasons – Summer sun, autumn leaves, winter snowfall (60 animated flakes!), and spring blossoms — all affecting your city's visuals.
Disasters That Challenge You – Survive thunderstorms with lightning bolts, fires with particle effects, earthquakes that shake your canvas, and floods that test your planning.
Smart Connection System – Connect buildings with roads, footpaths, power lines, and water pipes. Watch animated cars and dots travel along your networks!
Live City Dashboard – Track population, happiness, greenery, city score, and budget with real-time bar charts and analytics across 8 interactive tabs.
Save, Load & Undo – Save your city to browser storage, restore from history with thumbnail previews, and undo mistakes instantly (Ctrl+Z).
Immersive Ambient Music – Web Audio API generates dynamic city soundscapes that change with time of day — morning birds, afternoon bustle, evening jazz, and night ambience.
Zero Installation, One File – Just open the HTML file in any modern browser. No server, no database, no setup — pure front-end magic!
